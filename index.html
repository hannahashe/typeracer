How it works

Welcome to the Type Speed Test! Here’s how to play:

  1. Choose a difficulty from the Mode dropdown.
  2. Press Start to load a prompt.
  3. Begin typing — the timer starts on your first keystroke.
  4. Words typed correctly turn green; incorrect words turn red.
  5. Press Enter to end the test and see your results.
  6. Use Retry to try again with a new prompt.

Tip: Focus on accuracy first — speed will follow.
